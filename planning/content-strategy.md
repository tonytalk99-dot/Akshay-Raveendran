# Content Strategy, Editorial Tone & Brand Architecture

## 1. Brand Identity: AURA ARCHIVE
- **Brand Name:** AURA ARCHIVE (or `AURA Editions`)
- **Category:** Physical-Digital Spatial Art Foundry & Curated Collectibles
- **Brand Purpose:** Bridging physical materiality (machined aluminum, optical polycarbonate, precision UV varnishes) with interactive 3D spatial curation.
- **Brand Voice & Tone:**
  - **Museum-Grade & Contemplative:** Sophisticated, measured, and poetic.
  - **Cinematic & Archival:** Echoing prestigious independent cultural institutions (e.g., A24, Criterion Collection, MoMA Editions).
  - **Anti-Hype Luxury:** No garish sales copy, countdown timers, or crypto-slop jargon. Artworks speak with gravity, authorial intention, and material craftsmanship.
